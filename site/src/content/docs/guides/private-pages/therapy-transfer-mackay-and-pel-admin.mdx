### Archive old care channels

<Aside type="note">

On the Patients page, [activate the "With active channels" filter](/guides/for-healthcare-professionals/patient-files/filter-patient-files) to view all patients with the _2025-2026 RPSS - Therapy Transfer_ care channel. Archive those care channels.

</Aside>

<Aside type="note">

You can always access an archived care channel by clicking here. Parents can also access archived care channels in their app.

<h2 align="center"><img src="../.gitbook/assets/CleanShot 2026-08-17 at 13.54.40@2x.png" alt="" data-size="original" /></h2>

</Aside>

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

**In the patient file, identify the care channel and select&#x20;**_**See more**_**.**

<Figure src={img11} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Click on the box next to the title of the care channel.**

<Figure src={img12} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Select OK.**

<Figure src={img13} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>

## In the Admin App

### Remove access for professionals who have left

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

**In the admin app, open the&#x20;**_**Teams**_**&#x20;page.**

<Figure src={img14} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**1) Select the&#x20;**_**Members**_**&#x20;page, then clear 2)&#x20;**_**Participant**_**&#x20;and 3)&#x20;**_**Triaging**_**&#x20;for the person you wish to remove.**

<Figure src={img15} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Confirm here.**

<Figure src={img16} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**1) Select the&#x20;**_**Users**_**&#x20;page. 2) Select the three dots next to the person's name.**

<Figure src={img17} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Select&#x20;**_**Remove the user from the organizational unit**_**.**

<Figure src={img18} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**1) Enter the required text, then 2) click&#x20;**_**Delete permanently**_**.**

<Figure src={img19} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>
