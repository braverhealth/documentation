## The Problem

Your phone's adaptive battery feature can delay notifications. It limits background activity for apps you use infrequently in order to save battery.

This feature, found especially on Google Pixel phones, is often cited as the main cause of delayed notifications.

## The Solution

To fix this problem:

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

#### Go to Settings, then to the Battery section

<Figure src={img0} alt="Samsung Settings with Battery highlighted" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Then go to Battery protection

<Figure src={img1} alt="Battery settings with Battery protection highlighted" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Select the Basic option or turn the option off completely

<Figure src={img2} alt="Battery protection options with Basic selected" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>
