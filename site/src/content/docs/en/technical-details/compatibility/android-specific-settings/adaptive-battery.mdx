## The Problem

Your phone's adaptive battery feature can delay notifications. It limits background activity for apps you use infrequently in order to save battery.

This feature, found especially on Google Pixel phones, is often cited as the main cause of delayed notifications.

## The Solution

To fix this problem:

<ol>

<li>

#### Go to Settings, then to the Battery section

</li>

<li>

#### Then go to Battery protection
