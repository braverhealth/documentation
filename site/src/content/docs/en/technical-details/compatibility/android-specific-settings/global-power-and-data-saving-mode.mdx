### The Problem

Your notifications arrive late because power and data saving settings limit app activity in the background.

### The Solution

To get real-time notifications again, turn off these three settings:

1. Power saving mode
   * Go to ⁠Settings > Battery > Power saving
   * Turn the option off
2. Battery optimization (Samsung)
   * Go to ⁠Settings > Device care > Battery > Background usage limits
   * Turn optimization off
3. Data saver
   * Go to ⁠Settings > Connections > Data usage
   * Turn data saver off
