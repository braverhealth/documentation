## The Problem

The adaptive notifications feature uses artificial intelligence to manage the priority of your notifications based on your usage habits. Although it is designed to better organize your notifications, some users report unexpected delays caused by this feature.

## The Solution

To turn off adaptive notifications:

1. Go to ⁠**Settings > Apps & notifications**
2. Select ⁠**Special app access**
3. Tap ⁠**Adaptive notifications**
4. Select the ⁠**None** option
