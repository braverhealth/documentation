## The Problem

Some apps may have individual battery and data restrictions that prevent notifications from arriving in real time.

## The Solution

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

#### Press and hold the Braver icon, then tap the information icon ⓘ

<Figure src={img0} alt="Samsung home screen with the Braver app information menu open" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### In the app settings, go to ⁠Mobile data

<Figure src={img1} alt="Braver app information with Mobile data highlighted" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Turn on Allow background data usage

<Figure src={img2} alt="Braver app data usage with background data enabled" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Go back and open the Battery section

<Figure src={img3} alt="Braver app information with Battery highlighted" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Select the "Unrestricted" option

<Figure src={img4} alt="Braver battery options with Unrestricted selected" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>
