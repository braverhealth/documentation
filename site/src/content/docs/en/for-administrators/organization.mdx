title: Organization
icon: hospital
---

An **organization** is the legal entity that enters into a service agreement with Braver, and toward which Braver has obligations to manage patient health data securely and in compliance with regulations.

It encompasses not only workplaces and teams of healthcare workers, but also user management and monitoring through audit logs to ensure traceability.

The organization is responsible for protecting the confidentiality of the sensitive information stored in Braver.

Creating an organization is the first step required before forming teams and linking them to workplaces. **To do so, see the** [**configuration guide**](/en/guides/for-administrators/configuration-guide)**.**
