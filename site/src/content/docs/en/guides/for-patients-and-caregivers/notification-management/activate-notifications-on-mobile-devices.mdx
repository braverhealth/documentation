<Aside type="tip" icon="approve-check-circle">

Braver will support you more in your practice if we can notify you at the right time when a healthcare professional contacts you. Make sure your notifications are enabled by following this tutorial!

</Aside>

<Tabs>

<TabItem label="iPhone or iPad">

#### Step by Step

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

**On your mobile device, go to your** _**Settings**_.

<Figure src={img0} alt="iOS home screen with Settings highlighted" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Go to the "Notifications" section**

<Figure src={img1} alt="iOS Settings with Notifications highlighted" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Find the Braver application.**

<Figure src={img2} alt="iOS app notifications list with Braver highlighted" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Turn on the switch to** _**Allow Notifications**_.

Make sure the 3 display options are selected.

<Figure src={img2} alt="iOS app notifications list with Braver highlighted" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>

</TabItem>

<TabItem label="Android">

#### Step by Step

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

**On your mobile device, press and hold the Braver application icon and tap the information icon (i).**

<Figure src={img3} alt="Android home screen with Braver app information highlighted" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**You will be in the Braver application settings page. Go to the "Notifications" section.**

<Figure src={img4} alt="Android Braver app information with Notifications highlighted" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**In the notifications tab, make sure notifications are allowed with these settings.**

<Figure src={img5} alt="Android Braver notification settings enabled" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Another method to ensure notifications are active is to go to device settings.**

<Figure src={img6} alt="Android home screen with Settings highlighted" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**In settings, click on** _**Notifications**_ **and go to the list of** _**Application Notifications**_.

<Figure src={img7} alt="Android Settings with Notifications highlighted" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**At this point, make sure the Braver application switch is enabled.**

<Figure src={img8} alt="Android app notifications list with Braver enabled" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>

</TabItem>

</Tabs>
