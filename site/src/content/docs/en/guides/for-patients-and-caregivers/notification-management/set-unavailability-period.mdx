## Step by Step

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

#### Start by navigating to your profile at the bottom of the screen.

<Aside type="note">

On a mobile phone, you must tap the gear icon at the bottom of the screen.

</Aside>

<Figure src={img0} alt="Simone Duclos profile menu with Settings highlighted" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Click on the _Settings_ tab

<Figure src={img1} alt="Profile Settings tab highlighted" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Select _**Unavailability Period**_, where you can configure a period of unavailability during which you will not be notified. This will also leave a clear trace for users who want to reach you.

<Figure src={img2} alt="Unavailability period option highlighted in Settings" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Activate unavailable mode using the switch.

<Figure src={img3} alt="Unavailability period screen with the mode switch off" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Indicate the date and time of your return to work.

Then select the checkmark at the top right to save your choice.

<Figure src={img4} alt="Unavailability mode enabled with the return date and save button highlighted" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### If someone wants to invite you to a discussion, they will clearly see that you are unavailable.

<Figure src={img5} alt="Contact search showing Mathieu Toussaint unavailable until February 10" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>
