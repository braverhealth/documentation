## Step-by-Step

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

**Start by navigating to your profile at the bottom of the screen.**

<Aside type="note">

On a mobile phone, you must tap the gear icon at the bottom of the screen.

</Aside>

<Figure src={img0} alt="Simone Duclos profile menu with Settings highlighted" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Select Settings tab.

<Figure src={img1} alt="Mathieu Toussaint profile with Settings highlighted" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Under Security section, select Change My PIN.

<Figure src={img2} alt="Change my PIN option under Security" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Enter the new PIN.

<Figure src={img3} alt="New six-digit PIN keypad" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Repeat to ensure both PINs match.

<Aside type="tip" icon="approve-check-circle">

It is automatically saved!

</Aside>

<Figure src={img4} alt="Repeat six-digit PIN keypad" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>
