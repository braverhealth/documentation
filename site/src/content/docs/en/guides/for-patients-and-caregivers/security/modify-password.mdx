## Step-by-Step

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

**Start by navigating to your profile at the bottom of the screen.**

<Aside type="note">

On a mobile phone, you must tap the gear icon at the bottom of the screen.

</Aside>

<Figure src={img0} alt="Simone Duclos profile menu with Settings highlighted" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Select the _Settings_ tab.

<Figure src={img1} alt="Mathieu Toussaint profile with Settings highlighted" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Under Security, select _Change My Password_.

<Figure src={img2} alt="Change my password option under Security" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Enter the new password, then repeat to ensure both passwords match.

<Aside type="caution">

The password should contain letters, symbols, and numbers.

</Aside>

<Figure src={img3} alt="Password form with password and repeat password fields" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### When you are finished, click _Save_ at the top right.

<Figure src={img4} alt="Completed password form with Save highlighted" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>
