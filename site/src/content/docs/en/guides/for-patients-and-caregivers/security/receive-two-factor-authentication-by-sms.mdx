## Step-by-Step Guide

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

**Start by navigating to your profile at the bottom of the screen.**

<Aside type="note">

On a mobile phone, you must tap the gear icon at the bottom of the screen.

</Aside>

<Figure src={img0} alt="Simone Duclos profile menu with Settings highlighted" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Click on the _Settings_ tab.

<Figure src={img1} alt="Mathieu Toussaint profile with Settings highlighted" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Under Security, select _Two-Factor Authentication_.

<Figure src={img2} alt="Two-factor authentication option under Security" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### By default, your two-factor authentication is sent by email. If you want to receive it by SMS, select this option.

<Figure src={img3} alt="SMS option on the two-factor authentication screen" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### If you have no phone number associated with your account, select _Add Phone Number_.

<Figure src={img4} alt="Add a phone number for confirmation codes" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Enter your number and select _Create_.

<Figure src={img5} alt="Phone number entry with Create button" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### You will then receive a 6-digit confirmation code. Enter it in the appropriate field, then click _Validate_.

<Figure src={img6} alt="Confirmation code entry with Validate button" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>
