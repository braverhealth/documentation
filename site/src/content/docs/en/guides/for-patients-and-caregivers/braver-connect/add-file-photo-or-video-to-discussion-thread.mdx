## Step-by-Step

* #### **On a Computer**
  * [Add an attachment](/en/guides/for-patients-and-caregivers/braver-connect/add-file-photo-or-video-to-discussion-thread#on-a-computer)
* #### **On Mobile**
  * [Add an attachment](/en/guides/for-patients-and-caregivers/braver-connect/add-file-photo-or-video-to-discussion-thread#add-an-attachment-on-mobile)

### On a Computer

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

**In the discussion thread with your care team, choose one of the following:**

* **Click the "+" symbol to add a photo, video or file.**&#x20;
* **Click the paper clip icon to add a file. Go to step 3.**

<Figure src={img0} alt="Use the plus or paperclip in a desktop discussion" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**If you clicked "+", choose one of the following:**

* **Click&#x20;**_**Attach photo or video**_ **to share a photo or video with your care team.**
* **Click&#x20;**_**Attach file**_ **to share an image or any other document in PDF, DOC or MP3 format with your care team.**

<Figure src={img1} alt="Choose photo, video, or file on a computer" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**In your computer's folders, select the item you want to share and click&#x20;**_**Open**_**.**

<Figure src={img2} alt="Select a file in the computer file picker" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**At this point, you can still remove the item before sending it:**

1. **Click the three small dots to the right of the item.**
2. **Click&#x20;**_**Remove file**_**.**

<Figure src={img3} alt="Remove a pending file on a computer" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**To finish, tap the purple arrow to send your attachment.**

<Figure src={img4} alt="Send a pending attachment on a computer" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Your attachment has been sent!**

<Figure src={img5} alt="Sent attachment and paperclip count" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>

### Add an Attachment on Mobile

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

**In the discussion thread with your care team:**

* **Tap the "+" symbol to add a photo, video or file.**&#x20;
* **Tap the paper clip icon to add a file.**

<Figure src={img6} alt="Use the plus or paperclip in a mobile discussion" width={323} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**If you tapped "+", choose one of the following:**

1. **Tap&#x20;**_**Attach photo or video**_ **to share a photo or video with your care team.**
2. **Tap&#x20;**_**Attach file**_ **to share an image or any other document in PDF, DOC or MP3 format with your care team. Go to step 4.**

<Figure src={img7} alt="Choose a mobile attachment type" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**If you tapped&#x20;**_**Attach photo or video**_**, choose one of the following:**

1. **Tap&#x20;**_**Photo Library**_ **to share a photo or video from your device's camera roll with your care team.**
2. **Tap Take Photo or Video to take the photo right away. Go to the** [_**Take a Photo or Video on Mobile**_](/en/guides/for-patients-and-caregivers/braver-connect/add-file-photo-or-video-to-discussion-thread#take-a-photo-or-video-on-mobile) **section.**
3. **Tap&#x20;**_**Choose File**_ **to share an image or any other document in PDF, DOC or MP3 format with your care team.**

<Figure src={img8} alt="Choose a photo source or file" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Select the item you want to share and tap&#x20;**_**Open.**_

<Figure src={img9} alt="Select a photo on an iPhone" width={342} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**At this point, you can still remove the item before sending it:**

1. **Tap the three small dots to the right of the item.**
2. **Tap&#x20;**_**Remove file**_**.**

<Figure src={img10} alt="Remove a pending mobile file" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**To finish, tap the purple arrow to send your attachment.**

<Figure src={img11} alt="Send a pending mobile attachment" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Your attachment has been sent!**

<Figure src={img12} alt="Sent mobile attachment and paperclip count" width={323} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>

### Take a Photo or Video on Mobile

<Aside type="note">

A photo or video taken through Braver won't appear in your device's camera roll.

</Aside>

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

**In the discussion thread with your care team, tap the "+" symbol.**&#x20;

<Figure src={img6} alt="Use the plus or paperclip in a mobile discussion" width={323} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Tap&#x20;**_**Attach photo or video**_ **to share a photo or video with your care team.**

<Figure src={img13} alt="Attach a photo or video on mobile" width={282} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Tap&#x20;**_**Take Photo or Video.**_

<Figure src={img14} alt="Take a photo or video on mobile" width={284} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Your camera opens. Take your photo or video.**

<Figure src={img15} alt="Capture a photo with the iPhone camera" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**If you are happy with your photo, tap&#x20;**_**Use Photo**_**. Otherwise, tap&#x20;**_**Retake**_ **to go back to step 4.**

<Figure src={img16} alt="Use or retake the captured photo" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**At this point, you can still remove the item before sending it:**

1. **Tap the three small dots to the right of the item.**
2. **Tap&#x20;**_**Remove file**_**.**

<Figure src={img10} alt="Remove a pending mobile file" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**To finish, tap the purple arrow to send your photo or video.**

<Figure src={img11} alt="Send a pending mobile attachment" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Your photo or video has been sent!**

<Figure src={img12} alt="Sent mobile attachment and paperclip count" width={323} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>
