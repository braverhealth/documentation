## Step-by-Step

<Aside type="note">

You can create your account on a mobile device (phone or tablet) or a computer. You may have received an invitation by SMS or email.

</Aside>

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

**In the email you received, click the button to accept the invitation.**

<Aside type="caution">

**📱If you were invited by SMS, click the secure invitation link.**

</Aside>

<Figure src={img8} alt="Accept an invitation from email" width={375} />

</li>

<li>

**Is this your first time opening Braver?** Select _Create a new account_.

<Aside type="danger">

Is your screen showing something different? [Click here](/en/guides/for-patients-and-caregivers/accept-invitation#other-cases).

</Aside>

<Figure src={img0} alt="Invitation email with accept button" width={375} align="center" />

</li>

<li>

**Choose a password and repeat it. Then select&#x20;**_**Create my account**_**.**

<Aside type="caution">

**📱If you were invited by SMS, also fill in the email field. We'll send you a code to confirm your email address. (See next step)**

</Aside>

<Figure src={img1} alt="Choose a new account" width={375} align="center" />

</li>

<li>

<Aside type="caution">

**📱If you were invited by SMS, retrieve the 6-digit security code from your email and enter it in the text field.**

</Aside>

<Figure src={img9} alt="Find the one-time email security code" width={375} />

</li>

<li>

**Accept the terms of use.** Scroll to the bottom of the page to accept.

<Figure src={img2} alt="Create a password" width={375} align="center" />

</li>

<li>

**Set up a PIN to easily unlock your app.** Click on _Configure_.

<Figure src={img3} alt="Accept the English terms of service" width={375} align="center" />

</li>

<li>

**Create your PIN and repeat it. If you're on a computer, skip to step 10.**

<Figure src={img4} alt="Set up a PIN" width={262} align="center" />

</li>

## Other Cases

### First Case

If this is the first time you are opening Braver, but Braver has already been used by someone else on your device, you will see a screen like the following:

<Figure src={img5} alt="Choose an existing or new account" width={375} align="center" />

\
Next, select the "Create a new account" option to proceed to [step 3](/en/guides/for-patients-and-caregivers/accept-invitation#step-1). If you already have a Braver account, choose your name or select "Use an existing Braver account" to skip ahead to [step 10](/en/guides/for-patients-and-caregivers/accept-invitation#step-1).

### Second Case

If you have already created your account in the past with the same email address as the invitation, but your session is not already active on your device, you will see a screen like the following:

<Figure src={img6} alt="Sign in to an existing account" width={375} align="center" />

\
In this case, you should enter your password in order to proceed to [step 10](/en/guides/for-patients-and-caregivers/accept-invitation#step-by-step).

### Third Case

If you have already created your account in the past with the same email address as the invitation, and your session is already active on your device, you will see a screen like the following:

<Figure src={img7} alt="Enter a PIN to continue" width={375} align="center" />

\
In this case, you should simply enter your PIN to proceed to [step 10](/en/guides/for-patients-and-caregivers/accept-invitation#step-by-step).
