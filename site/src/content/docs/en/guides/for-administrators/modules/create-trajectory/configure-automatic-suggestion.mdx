Auto-suggestion determines when the trajectory is automatically suggested in patient files, based on the patient's status and a date range. This saves clicks during activation, and makes your teams autonomous if they are responsible for setting up a care channel for patients.

## Turn On Auto-suggestion

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

Select **Modules**, then the **Trajectories** tab, and choose the trajectory.

<Figure src={img19} alt="Trajectories tab of the Modules page" />

</li>

<li>

Select the **Auto-suggestion** tab, click **Configure auto-suggest rules**, then turn on the options you want in the **Auto-suggest rules** dialog.

<Figure src={img18} alt="Auto-suggestion options A to D" width={563} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>

## Options

| Marker | Option | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | When patient is active | The trajectory is suggested for all active patients. |
| B | When absent from patient | The trajectory is suggested unless a corresponding care channel already exists. |
| C | Before date | Suggestions stop appearing on this date, at midnight. |
| D | Starting on date | Suggestions start appearing on this date. |

<Aside type="tip">

You can turn on several options at once. For example, with A and B, the trajectory is suggested for active patients unless a corresponding care channel already exists.

</Aside>
