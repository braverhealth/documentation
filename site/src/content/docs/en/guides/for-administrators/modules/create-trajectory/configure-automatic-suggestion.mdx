Automatic suggestion determines when the trajectory is suggested in patient profiles, based on the patient's status and a date range. This saves clicks during activation, and makes your teams autonomous if they are responsible for setting up a care channel for patients.

## Turn On Automatic Suggestion

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

Select **Modules**, then the **Trajectories** tab, and choose the trajectory.

<Figure src={img19} alt="Trajectories tab of the Modules page" />

</li>

<li>

Select the **Automatic suggestion** tab, then turn on the options you want.

<Figure src={img18} alt="Automatic suggestion tab, options A to D" width={563} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>

## Options

| Marker | Option | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | When the patient is active | The trajectory is suggested for all active patients. |
| B | When missing from the patient | The trajectory is suggested automatically, except for patients who already have the matching care channel. |
| C | Before date | The date until which the trajectory is suggested in the patient's profile. |
| D | From date | The date from which the trajectory is suggested in the patient's profile. |

<Aside type="tip">

You can turn on several options at once.

</Aside>
