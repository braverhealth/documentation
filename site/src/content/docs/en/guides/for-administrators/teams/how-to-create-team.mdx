## Step-by-Step

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

#### Access the [administrative console](https://admin.braver.net)

</li>

<li>

#### Click Teams in the left menu

<Figure src={img0} alt="Teams selected in the administration menu" width={563} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Click on the add button at the bottom right

<Figure src={img1} alt="Add button at the lower right of the teams list" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Enter the information

* Enter the team name
* Enter the team description
* Associate the team with one or more workplaces
* Click Create
