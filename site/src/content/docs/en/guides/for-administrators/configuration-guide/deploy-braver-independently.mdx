### Here are the steps to set up your organization in Braver:

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

#### Define Your Organization Structure

If you are using Braver independently and are not sure about the best structure for your organization, let us help you.

Here is a concrete example of a healthcare organization structure:

<img src="../../.gitbook/assets/file.excalidraw (1).svg" alt="Private Physiotherapy Clinic" class="gitbook-drawing" />

</li>

<li>

#### Submit an Organization Creation Request

1. Share your organization's name with the Braver team using the following form
2. Wait for a Braver team member to contact you for the next steps

<Embed url="https://braverhealth.typeform.com/to/smKccheM">[https://braverhealth.typeform.com/to/smKccheM](https://braverhealth.typeform.com/to/smKccheM)</Embed>

</li>

<li>

#### Access the Administrative Application

<Aside type="caution">

If you have not yet created your account, you should have received an email invitation. Here is [the tutorial](/en/guides/for-healthcare-professionals/account-creation/independent-account-creation) to help you create your account.

</Aside>

You can access the administrative application via [https://admin.braver.net](https://admin.braver.net/#/login). Use the same credentials as those for logging into your Braver account (email + password)

</li>

<li>

#### Create Your Organizational Units

Here is [how to create an organizational unit](/en/guides/for-administrators/organizational-units/how-to-create-organizational-unit).

If you are not familiar with the concept of organizational unit, here is [the documentation](/en/guides/for-administrators/organizational-units).

</li>

<li>

#### Create Your Workplaces

Here is [how to create a workplace](/en/guides/for-administrators/workplaces/how-to-create-workplace).

If you are not familiar with the concept of workplace, here is [the documentation](/en/guides/for-administrators/workplaces).

</li>

<li>

#### Create Your Teams

Here is [how to create a team](/en/guides/for-administrators/teams/how-to-create-team).

If you are not familiar with the concept of team, here is [the documentation](/en/guides/for-administrators/teams).

</li>

<li>

#### Send User Invitations

Before sending user invitations, it is very important to understand what roles and permissions you will grant them. Here is [the documentation](/en/for-administrators/users/roles-and-permissions) to help you.

You are now ready to send user invitations. Here is [how to invite a user](/en/guides/for-administrators/users/how-to-invite-users).

<Aside type="note">

For inviting patients and caregivers, this is done by authorized clinicians through the clinician application. Here is [a tutorial](/en/guides/for-healthcare-professionals/patient-caregiver-communication/invite-patient-or-caregiver-to-join-braver) to guide you.

</Aside>

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" icon="approve-check-circle">

**Congratulations, your organization is now part of the Braver network!** 🎉

There are only two sections left to explore:

* [Patient and Caregiver Module](/en/guides/for-administrators/configuration-guide#le-module-patient-et-proche-aidant) (if relevant to you)
* [Other Technical Details](/en/guides/for-administrators/configuration-guide#autres-details-techniques) (if relevant to you)

</Aside>
