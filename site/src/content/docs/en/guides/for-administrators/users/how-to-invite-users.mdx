## Step-by-Step

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

#### Access the [admin console](https://admin.braver.net)

</li>

<li>

#### Click Users in the left menu

<Figure src={img0} alt="Users selected in the administration menu" width={563} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Click on the add button at the bottom right

<Figure src={img1} alt="Add button at the lower right of the users list" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Enter the information

* Enter the first and last name
* Enter the email address
* Select applicable roles and permissions

<Figure src={img2} alt="Invite a new user form with personal details and available roles" width={1200} align="left" />

<a href={img2.src} target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Open the full-size invitation form screenshot</a>.

</li>

<li>

#### Select the team where the user will be located

* By selecting **Participant** so the person is part of the team as a participant in discussions and care channels
* By selecting **Triage** so the person has access to the team's [triage box](/en/guides/for-healthcare-professionals/teams) (if relevant)

<Figure src={img3} alt="Teams list with Participant and Triage selected for the Test team" width={1200} align="left" />

<a href={img3.src} target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Open the full-size team selection screenshot</a>.

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>
