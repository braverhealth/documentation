## Step-by-Step

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

#### Click on Team tab to access your team's triage box.

<Figure src={img0} alt="Team navigation button with one unread item" width={60} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Select Triage to view the new message received by your team.

<Figure src={img1} alt="Select the team Triage tab" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Click on the new message to open it. New messages are marked with a red circle and are highlighted.

<Figure src={img2} alt="Open the new triage thread" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### You will need to choose Accept and Assign to respond yourself.

<Figure src={img3} alt="Accept and assign the thread to yourself" width={563} align="left" />

<Aside type="tip" icon="approve-check-circle">

You can now reply to the discussion thread!

</Aside>

</li>

<li>

#### It will no longer appear in the triage box, but will be in your Active Threads under the Home tab.

<Figure src={img4} alt="Empty triage view after the thread is assigned" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>
