## Step-by-Step

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

#### Start a new discussion thread by clicking on the bubble on the home page.

<Figure src={img0} alt="Start a new discussion from Home" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Choose the type of discussion thread that suits you, then in the participants section, click _Invite_.

<Figure src={img1} alt="Invite participants in a new discussion" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### To invite a team, click on the workplace of the team in question.

<Figure src={img2} alt="Choose a workplace from the directory" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Under the workplace name, click _Add to Discussion_.

<Figure src={img3} alt="Add a workplace team to the discussion" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Select the desired team.

<Figure src={img3} alt="Add the selected team to the discussion" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Here we can see that the IPS team has been selected (see orange box).

Finish writing your message and send it by clicking here.

<Figure src={img4} alt="Compose a message to the IPS team" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>
