<Aside type="note">

If you aren't ready to invite a patient but have all the information needed to fill out the invitation form, you can prefill the patient or caregiver information.

</Aside>

## Step-by-Step

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

In the patient record, click the purple rectangle at the top right. The rectangle can show different symbols depending on where you are in the invitation process.

<Figure src={img0} alt="" />

</li>

<li>

Click the plus sign with a person icon, to the right of the _Patient_ option OR to the right of the _Responsible person_ option, depending on who you want to invite.

<Figure src={img1} alt="" />

</li>

<li>

Fill in the required fields.

<Aside type="caution">

Make sure that _Patient_ OR _Responsible person_ is checked under _Team member_, depending on who you want to invite.

</Aside>

<Figure src={img2} alt="" />

</li>

<li>

Click _Save._&#x20;

<Aside type="caution">

Don't turn on _Invite now_.

</Aside>

<Figure src={img3} alt="" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>

<Aside type="note">

* An envelope to the right of the invitee's name indicates a pending invitation.&#x20;
* Once they have joined Braver, the envelope is replaced by a blue check mark.&#x20;
* If you fill out the invitee's record without sending the invitation, a dotted envelope is shown.
