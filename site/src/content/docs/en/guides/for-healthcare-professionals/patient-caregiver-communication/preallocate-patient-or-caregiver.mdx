<Aside type="note">

In a care channel, all discussions are visible to all participants. If you are ready to start using a care channel but don't yet have the information needed to invite a patient or their circle of care, you can _pre-allocate_ them.

The _Pre-allocate_ action then lets you add them to a care channel even if they haven't joined Braver yet. As soon as they have a Braver account, they will have access to all the information in the care channel where they were pre-allocated.

</Aside>

## Step-by-Step

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

In the patient record, click the purple rectangle at the top right. The rectangle can show different symbols depending on where you are in the invitation process.

<Figure src={img0} alt="" />

</li>

<li>

Under Patient or Responsible persons, click Pre-allocate.

<Figure src={img1} alt="" />

</li>

<li>

You can now add the patient or the responsible persons to a care channel. As soon as they have a Braver account, they will have access to all the information in the care channel where they were pre-allocated.

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>
