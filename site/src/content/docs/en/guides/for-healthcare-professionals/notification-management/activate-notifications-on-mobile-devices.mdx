<Aside type="tip" icon="approve-check-circle">

Braver will support you more in your practice if we are able to notify you at the right time when a healthcare professional contacts you. Make sure your notifications are enabled by following this tutorial!

</Aside>

<Tabs>

<TabItem label="iPhone or iPad">

#### Step by Step

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

**On your mobile device, go to your _Settings_.**

<Figure src={img0} alt="iOS Settings app icon" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Go to the "Notifications" section**

<Figure src={img1} alt="iOS Settings list with Notifications highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Find the Braver application.**

<Figure src={img2} alt="iOS Notifications app list with Braver highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Enable the switch to _Allow Notifications_.**

Make sure the 3 display options are selected.

<Figure src={img2} alt="Braver entry in the iOS Notifications app list" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>

</TabItem>

<TabItem label="Android">

#### Step by Step

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

**On your mobile device, press and hold the Braver application icon and tap the information icon (i).**

<Figure src={img3} alt="Android Braver app menu with its information button highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**You will be in the Braver application settings page. Go to the "Notifications" section.**

<Figure src={img4} alt="Android Braver app info with Notifications highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**In the notifications tab, make sure notifications are allowed with these settings.**

<Figure src={img5} alt="Android Braver notification settings with alerts enabled" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Another method to ensure notifications are active is to go to device settings.**

<Figure src={img6} alt="Android home screen with the Settings app highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**In settings, click on _Notifications_ and go to the _Application Notifications_ list.**

<Figure src={img7} alt="Android Notifications settings with App notifications highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**At this point, make sure the Braver application switch is enabled.**

<Figure src={img8} alt="Android App notifications list with Braver's enabled switch highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>

</TabItem>

</Tabs>
