## Step by Step

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

#### Start by navigating to your profile at the bottom of the screen.

<Figure src={img0} alt="Profile menu with Camille Lavoie's profile highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Click on the _Settings_ tab.

<Figure src={img1} alt="Camille Lavoie's profile with the Settings tab highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Select _**Availability Schedule**_. According to this schedule, your notifications will be paused until your return, when you will be informed of what you missed.

<Figure src={img2} alt="Profile settings with Availability schedule highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### To modify your availability schedule, click on the dropdown menu to see the choices.

<Figure src={img3} alt="Availability screen with the schedule dropdown highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### In this example, the user wants a custom work schedule.

<Figure src={img4} alt="Availability options with Custom highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### For a custom schedule, indicate the hours during which you want to receive notifications for each day of the week.

<Figure src={img5} alt="Custom availability hours for weekdays" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Save your schedule in the top right corner.

<Figure src={img6} alt="Custom availability schedule with Save highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>
