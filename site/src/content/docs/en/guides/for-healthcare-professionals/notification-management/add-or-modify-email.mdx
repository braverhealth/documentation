<Aside type="note">

Braver sends you new message notifications and weekly summaries by email. If you sign in through one of our integration partners, your email may not be shared with Braver; you can add it manually.&#x20;

</Aside>

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

### Click your profile avatar.

<Figure src={img0} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

### Click your name.

<Figure src={img1} alt="" width={372} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

### Select the _Settings_ tab.

<Figure src={img2} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

### Under Communications, click _Email(s)_.

<Figure src={img3} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

### Click _Add_.

<Figure src={img4} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

<Aside type="note">

If you want to replace an existing email with a new one, the page will look like this instead:

<img src="../../.gitbook/assets/CleanShot 2026-03-02 at 16.36.23@2x.png" alt="" data-size="original" />

</Aside>

</li>

<li>

### Enter your email, then click _Add this email_.

<Figure src={img5} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

### A validation code is sent to your email. Open your inbox and look for an email titled _Braver | Security code for new email address_.

<Figure src={img6} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

### Copy the security code from the email.

<Figure src={img7} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

### Go back to the add email page and paste the code in the field provided.

<Figure src={img8} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

### Click _Validate_ to finish adding the email.

<Figure src={img9} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

### Click _Set as primary_.

<Aside type="note">

Set it as primary if you want this email to replace an older one, or if it is the first email added to your settings!

</Aside>

<Figure src={img10} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

### Your email now appears with the _Primary_ label.

<Figure src={img11} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>
