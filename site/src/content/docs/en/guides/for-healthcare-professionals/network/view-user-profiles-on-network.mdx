## Step-by-Step

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

**Go to the Network page.**

<Figure src={img0} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Select the person whose profile you want to view.**

<Figure src={img1} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**If the person does not appear in your contacts, use the search icon and type their name to find them.**

<Figure src={img2} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Once you find the person, click their name to open their profile.**

<Figure src={img3} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**In their profile, select Detailed profile.**

<Figure src={img4} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Depending on what the person has set up, you will be able to view their bio, their profession (if they are on Braver’s external network), and their workplace.**

<Figure src={img5} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>
