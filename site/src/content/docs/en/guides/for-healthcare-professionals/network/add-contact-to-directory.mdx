## Step-by-Step Guide

<Aside type="note">

Adding a contact to your directory creates a shortcut that makes it easier to add this person when creating a discussion thread. Make sure to add your most frequent collaborators to your directory!

</Aside>

<Aside type="tip" icon="approve-check-circle">

All your friends will be automatically added to your directory.

</Aside>

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

**Go to the&#x20;**_**Network**_**&#x20;page.**

<Figure src={img0} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Search for the participant you want to add to your directory, by clicking on the magnifying glass.**

<Figure src={img1} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Select the participant to open their profile.**

<Figure src={img2} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Click Add to My Directory.**

<Figure src={img3} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**The contact is now added to my directory. I can remove them if needed.**

<Figure src={img4} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**All participants in my directory are displayed on the&#x20;**_**Network**_**&#x20;page under&#x20;**_**Contacts**_**.**

<Figure src={img5} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>
