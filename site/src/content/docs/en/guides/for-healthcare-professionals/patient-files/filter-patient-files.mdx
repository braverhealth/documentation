<Aside type="note">

Filters let you [filter patient record](/en/guides/for-healthcare-professionals/patient-files/filter-patient-files#id-1.-filter-patient-records)s and [view the status of their invitation process](/en/guides/for-healthcare-professionals/patient-files/filter-patient-files#id-2.-view-the-invitation-process-status).

</Aside>

## 1. Filter patient records

### Step-by-step

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

**Go to the Patients page, then select Filters.**

<Figure src={img0} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Choose one or more of those options.**

<Aside type="note">

Filters vary depending on the roles assigned to you and the configurations set by your establishment.

</Aside>

<Figure src={img1} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Click Save.**

<Figure src={img2} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>

## 2. View the invitation process status

### Step-by-step

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

**1) Go to the Patients page, then 2) select Filters.**

<Figure src={img0} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Turn on Show the patient’s circle to access the dropdown.**

<Figure src={img4} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Select one or more of those options:**

<Figure src={img5} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Then select a filter to display the patients you are looking for:**

<Figure src={img6} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Click Save.**

<Figure src={img2} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>
