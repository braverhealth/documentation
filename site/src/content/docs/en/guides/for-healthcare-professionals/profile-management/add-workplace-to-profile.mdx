### Add a Workplace to My Profile

<Aside type="note">

You can have more than one workplace!

</Aside>

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

**1) Open your profile from your avatar at the bottom of the screen, then 2) click your name.**

<Figure src={img0} alt="Open your profile from the avatar menu" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Scroll to the bottom of the page and click&#x20;**_**Join a workplace**_**.**

<Figure src={img1} alt="Join a workplace option in your profile" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Use the search bar to find your workplace.**

<Figure src={img2} alt="Search for a workplace by name" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Does your workplace already exist in Braver? Select it as in the example. Otherwise, see the** [_**Create a New Workplace in the Braver Network**_](/en/guides/for-healthcare-professionals/profile-management/add-workplace-to-profile#create-a-new-workplace-in-the-braver-network) **section to continue.**

<Figure src={img3} alt="Select an existing workplace from search results" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Then click&#x20;**_**Join**_**.**

<Figure src={img4} alt="Join the selected workplace" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**1) Confirm that you work at this location, 2) enter the start date and click&#x20;**_**Join this workplace.**_

<Figure src={img5} alt="Confirm your workplace and start year" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**You now appear in the list of professionals who work at this location.**

<Figure src={img6} alt="Professionals listed at your workplace" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>

<Aside type="note">

Your new workplace also appears in your profile.

</Aside>

<Figure src={img7} alt="New workplace listed on your profile" width={375} align="left" />

### Create a New Workplace in the Braver Network

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

**If the workplace hasn't been created in Braver yet, click&#x20;**_**Create a new workplace**_**.**

<Figure src={img8} alt="Create a new workplace from search results" width={251} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**1) Type the workplace you want to create in the search box at the top of the page. One or more Google Maps suggestions appear in the menu under the search bar. 2) Select the right one.**

<Aside type="note">

You can also enter an address manually. See the green box in the image below.

</Aside>

<Figure src={img9} alt="Select a workplace address or enter it manually" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**You can 1) add a unit if needed, then 2) click Next.**

<Figure src={img10} alt="Add a unit and continue" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Select a category from the drop-down menu.**

<Figure src={img11} alt="Select a workplace category" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Click&#x20;**_**Next**_**.**

<Figure src={img12} alt="Hospital category selected and Next button" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**1) Confirm that you work at this location, 2) enter the start date and 3) click&#x20;**_**Next.**_

<Figure src={img13} alt="Confirm workplace and choose a start year" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**You now appear in the list of professionals who work at this location.**

<Figure src={img6} alt="Professionals listed at your workplace" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>
