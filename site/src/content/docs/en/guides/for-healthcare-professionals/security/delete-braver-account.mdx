## Step by Step

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

#### Select the badge with your photo or initials to access your profile, then click on your name.

<Figure src={img0} alt="Open your profile from the avatar menu" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Click on the _Settings_ tab.

<Figure src={img1} alt="Select the Settings tab" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### At the bottom of the page, go to _Delete Your Account_.

<Figure src={img2} alt="Select Delete your account from Settings" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Click on _Delete Account_.

<Figure src={img3} alt="Select Delete account" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Write _braver_ in the appropriate space and confirm by clicking _Delete Account_ one last time.

<Figure src={img4} alt="Type braver to confirm account deletion" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>
