## Step-by-Step

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

#### Select the badge with your photo or initials to access your profile, then click on your name.

<Figure src={img0} alt="Open your profile from the avatar menu" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Select the _Settings_ tab.

<Figure src={img1} alt="Select the Settings tab" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Under Security, select _Change My Password_.

<Figure src={img2} alt="Select Change my password under Security" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Enter the new password, then repeat to ensure both passwords match.

<Aside type="caution">

The password must contain letters, symbols, and numbers.

</Aside>

<Figure src={img3} alt="Enter and repeat a new password" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### When finished, click _Save_ in the top right corner.

<Figure src={img4} alt="Save the new password" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>
