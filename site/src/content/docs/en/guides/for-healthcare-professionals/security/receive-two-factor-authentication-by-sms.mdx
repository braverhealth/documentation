## Step-by-Step

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

#### Select the badge with your photo or initials to access your profile, then click on your name.

<Figure src={img0} alt="Open your profile from the avatar menu" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Click on the _Settings_ tab.

<Figure src={img1} alt="Select the Settings tab" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Under Security, select _Two-Factor Authentication_.

<Figure src={img2} alt="Select Two-factor authentication under Security" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### By default, your two-factor authentication is sent by email. If you want to receive it by SMS, select this option.

<Figure src={img3} alt="Choose SMS rather than email for login codes" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### If you have no phone number associated with your account, select _Add a Phone Number_.

<Figure src={img4} alt="Add a phone number for SMS codes" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Enter your number and select _Create_.

<Figure src={img5} alt="Enter the phone number and select Create" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### You will then receive a 6-digit confirmation code. Enter it in the appropriate field, then click _Validate_.

<Figure src={img6} alt="Enter the six-digit confirmation code" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>
