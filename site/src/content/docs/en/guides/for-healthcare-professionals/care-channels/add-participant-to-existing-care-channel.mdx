## Step by Step

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

#### In the patient file concerned, locate the care channel for which you want to add a participant. Click _View Details_.

<Figure src={img0} alt="Care channel card with View details highlighted" width={563} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Click on the participant avatars at the top right.

<Figure src={img1} alt="Care channel details with participant avatars highlighted" width={563} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### To add an individual participant (not a team), click here.

<Figure src={img2} alt="Participants screen with Add for individual participants highlighted" width={563} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Select the desired participant. If they do not appear automatically, search for them using the search bar.

<Figure src={img3} alt="Contacts list with Lucy Garner selected" width={563} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Click _Add to Discussion_, then confirm your choice.

<Figure src={img4} alt="Lucy Garner profile with Add to discussion highlighted" width={563} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>
