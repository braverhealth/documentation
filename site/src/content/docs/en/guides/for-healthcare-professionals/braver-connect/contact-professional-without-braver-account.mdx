### Create a Braver Connect Contact

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

**Go to the Network page.**

<Figure src={img0} alt="Home page with Network navigation highlighted" width={563} align="left" />

<Aside type="note">

This step-by-step guide shows you how to create a Braver Connect contact from the Network page. Since there are several ways to do this, you can also create this type of contact while writing a discussion thread: Add a participant > Add a contact.


</Aside>

</li>

<li>

**If this is the first time you are working with this person, click&#x20;**_**Add a contact**_**.**

<Figure src={img1} alt="My Network page with Add a contact highlighted" width={563} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Fill out the&#x20;**_**Add an external person**_ **form:**

1. Their title
2. Their first name
3. Their last name
4. Their email address

<Aside type="note">

Email address: turn on the switch under the email if it is a general address, in case you don't know the email of the contact you want to add.

</Aside>

5. Their profession
6. Their workplace ([See the step-by-step for this action in the _Add a Workplace_ section.](/en/guides/for-healthcare-professionals/braver-connect/contact-professional-without-braver-account#add-a-workplace))

<Aside type="danger">

Invite now: leave the switch off.

</Aside>

<Figure src={img2} alt="Add an external person form with six numbered fields and Invite now off" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Click&#x20;**_**Create an external contact**_**.**

<Figure src={img3} alt="Create an external contact button after choosing a workplace" width={563} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Once your contact is created, it appears on your Network page. Its avatar has a dotted outline because the person doesn't have a Braver account yet.**

<Figure src={img4} alt="My Network list with external contacts outlined" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>

### Share a Thread with a Braver Connect Contact by Email

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

**The avatars of your "Braver Connect" contacts appear with a dotted outline on the Network page. 1) Click the contact, then 2) click&#x20;**_**New thread**_**.**

<Figure src={img5} alt="External contact profile with New thread highlighted" width={750} align="left" />

<Aside type="note">

There are several ways to write to a Braver Connect contact. You can also start a discussion thread from the _Home_ page. When you add participants to the thread, you can select your Braver Connect contact the same way as your "regular" contacts.


</Aside>

</li>

<li>

**Turn on the&#x20;**_**Send by email**_ **option.**

<Figure src={img6} alt="Share options with Send by email highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**The email you entered when you filled out the&#x20;**_**Add an external person**_ **form is already displayed in the right field.**

<Figure src={img7} alt="Required email address for sharing a secure thread" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Turn on the switch under the email if it is a general address, in case you don't know the email of the contact you want to add.**

<Figure src={img8} alt="Company-email switch selected for a general address" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**By default, the person who receives a Braver Connect email receives a secret code to use as a password. If you prefer to use a security question and answer, turn on the following option:**

<Aside type="note">

If you turned on the switch stating that "This email identifies a business or organization, rather than a person", you can't use a security question and answer.

</Aside>

<Figure src={img9} alt="Security-question switch for access without a secret code" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

1. **Write a security question for your recipient.**
2. **Enter the answer to this question.**
3. **Repeat the answer to confirm it.**

<Figure src={img10} alt="Security question, answer and confirmation fields" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Click&#x20;**_**Share thread**_**.**

<Figure src={img11} alt="Share thread button for email delivery" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Complete the discussion thread as you usually do. The Braver Connect contact appears among the participants. Add any missing participants as needed. Click&#x20;**_**Send**_**.**

<Figure src={img12} alt="Send button with the external contact listed as a participant" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>

### Share a Thread with a Braver Connect Contact by Fax

<Aside type="note">

The fax contains the following information:

* Your name and profession
* Your workplace and its address
* The name and profession of the person the fax is intended for
* Their workplace and its address
* The fax number
* The patient's initials and age (for a clinical thread)
* Instructions to join the discussion thread. There are 2 options:
  * Scan a QR code
  * Go to a URL

</Aside>

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

**The avatars of your "Braver Connect" contacts appear with a dotted outline on the Network page. 1) Click the contact, then 2) click&#x20;**_**New thread**_**.**

<Figure src={img5} alt="External contact profile with New thread highlighted" width={750} align="left" />

<Aside type="note">

There are several ways to write to a Braver Connect contact. You can also start a discussion thread from the _Home_ page. When you add participants to the thread, you can select your Braver Connect contact the same way as your "regular" contacts.


</Aside>

</li>

<li>

**Turn on the&#x20;**_**Send by fax**_ **option.**

<Figure src={img13} alt="Send by fax option highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Enter the fax number in the right field.**

<Figure src={img14} alt="Required fax number field for thread sharing" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Click&#x20;**_**Share thread**_**.**

<Figure src={img15} alt="Share thread button for fax delivery" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Complete the discussion thread as you usually do. The Braver Connect contact appears among the participants. Add any missing participants as needed. Click&#x20;**_**Send**_**.**

<Figure src={img12} alt="Send button with the external contact listed as a participant" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>

### Add a Workplace

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

**Click&#x20;**_**Select workplace**_**.**

<Figure src={img16} alt="Select workplace in the external-person form" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Search to check whether the workplace already exists in the Braver network.**

<Figure src={img17} alt="Search for a workplace by name on Braver" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**If it exists, select it in the menu. Otherwise, go to step 5.**

<Figure src={img18} alt="Existing workplace in search results highlighted" width={500} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Click&#x20;**_**Select workplace.**_**&#x20;The workplace has been added. You don't need to follow the next steps of this guide.**

<Figure src={img19} alt="Select workplace button on an existing workplace profile" width={500} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**If the workplace hasn't been created in Braver yet, click&#x20;**_**Create a new workplace**_**.**

<Figure src={img20} alt="Create a new workplace option in search results" width={500} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**1) Type the workplace you want to create in the search box at the top of the page. One or more Google Maps suggestions appear in the menu under the search bar. 2) Select the right one.**

<Aside type="note">

You can also enter an address manually. See the green box in the image below.

</Aside>

<Figure src={img21} alt="Workplace address suggestions and manual-entry option" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**You can 1) add a unit if needed, then 2) click Next.**

<Figure src={img22} alt="Optional workplace unit field and Next button" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Select a category from the drop-down menu.**

<Figure src={img23} alt="Hospital category highlighted in the workplace category menu" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Click&#x20;**_**Next**_**.**

<Figure src={img24} alt="Next button after selecting the Hospital category" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Review the information and click&#x20;**_**Create the workplace**_**.**

<Figure src={img25} alt="Create workplace button after reviewing its details" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>
