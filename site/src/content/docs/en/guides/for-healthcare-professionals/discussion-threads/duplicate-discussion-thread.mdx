## Step-by-Step

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

#### While writing a new message or in a draft, click on the three small dots.

<Figure src={img0} alt="Discussion thread draft with the more options button at the top" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Select _Duplicate this draft_.

<Figure src={img1} alt="Draft menu with Duplicate this draft highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### The message has been duplicated and now appears in your active threads. You can duplicate it as many times as necessary.

<Figure src={img2} alt="Active threads list with the duplicated Follow-up draft highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Note that the patient and participant associated with the original thread are not included in the copy.

<Figure src={img3} alt="Duplicated draft without its original patient or participants" width={650} align="left" />

You can therefore associate it with the patient of your choice as needed and with new participants.

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>
