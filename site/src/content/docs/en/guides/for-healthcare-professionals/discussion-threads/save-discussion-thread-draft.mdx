## Step-by-Step

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

#### Create a new discussion thread.

<Figure src={img0} alt="Active discussion list with the create-thread button highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### If you do not have time to complete your message, click _Save draft_.

<Figure src={img1} alt="New thread form with Save draft highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### The draft will appear in your active threads. You will recognize it by the pencil image in the participant bubble.

<Figure src={img2} alt="Active threads list showing the new draft with a pencil icon" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### You can delete it by opening it and clicking on the three small dots, then _Delete draft_.

#### You can also save it again or duplicate it to send it to other participants.

<Figure src={img3} alt="Draft menu with Delete draft, Save draft and close, and Duplicate this draft" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>
