## Scenario 1: Add one or more participants

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

**Go to the&#x20;**_**Home**_**&#x20;page.**

<Figure src={img0} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Select the bubble icon at the bottom of the page.**

<Figure src={img1} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Choose Clinical Thread.**

<Figure src={img2} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Select Choose a Patient.**

<Figure src={img3} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**1) Use the search bar to find the relevant patient. 2) Click the desired patient.**

<Figure src={img4} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Check the record to make sure it is the correct patient. Then click&#x20;**_**Create in this patient**_**.**&#x20;

<Figure src={img5} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Select an existing care channel or choose&#x20;**_**Outside of any channel**_**. In this example,&#x20;**_**Outside of any channel**_**&#x20;is selected.**

<Figure src={img6} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**In the composition menu:**

1. **Enter a title for your message.**
2. **Write the message content.**
3. **Select&#x20;**_**Attachments**_**&#x20;to add files if needed.**
4. **Set the priority to urgent if needed.**
5. **Click&#x20;**_**Next**_**&#x20;to go to the second composition page.**

<Aside type="note">

At this stage, if you selected a care channel in step 7, you will not be able to edit the participants, since they are predefined by the care channel. You will be able to do it once your message has been sent.

See the tutorial [_Manage Discussion Thread Participants_](/en/guides/for-healthcare-professionals/discussion-threads/manage-discussion-thread-participants) for more details.

</Aside>

<Figure src={img7} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Click&#x20;**_**Add a participant**_**.**

<Figure src={img8} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

1. &#x20;**Use the search bar to find the participants you want.**&#x20;
2. **Click the name of the person you want to add.**

<Aside type="note">

To add a team, see [_Scenario 2: Add one or more teams_](/en/guides/for-healthcare-professionals/discussion-threads/create-clinical-discussion-thread#scenario-2-add-one-or-more-teams) below.

</Aside>

<Figure src={img9} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**After selecting the participant, click&#x20;**_**Add to the discussion**_**.**

<Figure src={img10} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Click&#x20;**_**Send**_**&#x20;at the bottom of the page.**

<Figure src={img11} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" icon="approve-check-circle">

**Where to find them:**

* Clinical threads are available from the _Home_ page. They are the ones linked to a patient record and shown in purple.
* You can also find clinical threads from the _Patients_ page by selecting the relevant patient.
* You can also find clinical threads from the _Network_ page by selecting one of your collaborators in the thread.

</Aside>

## Scenario 2: Add one or more teams

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

**In step 10 of Scenario 1, click the workplace where your team is located:**

* **Use the search bar to find the desired workplace.**
* **Click the name of the workplace where the team you want to add is located.**

<Figure src={img12} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**After selecting the workplace, click&#x20;**_**Add to the discussion**_**.**

<Figure src={img13} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Click the name of the team you want to add.**

<Figure src={img14} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Click&#x20;**_**Send**_**&#x20;at the bottom of the page.**

<Figure src={img15} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>
