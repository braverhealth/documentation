## Step by Step

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

#### If you receive a discussion thread for a patient not yet associated with your patient file bank, the icon will be gray.

<Figure src={img0} alt="Active thread showing a patient not yet linked to a file" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### You can then _Accept_ or _Refuse_ to respond to the discussion thread. Notice the orange mention about the patient file.

<Figure src={img1} alt="New discussion about Marion Deschamps with the Accept button highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### To link the discussion thread to the correct patient, you will need to click on the patient file at the top of the thread.

<Figure src={img2} alt="Discussion thread with the unlinked patient header highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Select _Associate with a patient_.

<Figure src={img3} alt="Patient header menu with Associate with a patient highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### In this case, the patient is not in your patient file bank.

If this had been the case, you could have selected her from your patient files.

You must therefore create a new patient file.

Click on the icon at the bottom right.

<Figure src={img4} alt="No patient found for Marion Deschamps with the create-patient button highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Validate the information and modify it if necessary.

Then save by clicking on the checkmark at the top right.

<Figure src={img5} alt="New patient form for Marion Deschamps with the save checkmark highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### The patient file is now created!

<Figure src={img6} alt="Discussion thread now linked to Marion Deschamps's patient file" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>
