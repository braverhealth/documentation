## Step-by-Step Guide to Adding a Participant from Workplace

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

#### Click on the participant circle at the top right of your thread.

<Figure src={img0} alt="Participant circle at the top right of a discussion thread" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### If you want to add an individual participant, click _Add +_ to the right of _Individual Participants_.

<Figure src={img1} alt="Participants panel with Add for individual participants highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### You can search for a participant by finding their workplace. Use the search bar at the top if needed. Click on the workplace.

<Figure src={img2} alt="Workplace picker with Centre de réadaptation Soleil highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Once in the correct workplace, click on the team in which the participant is involved.

<Figure src={img3} alt="Workplace team list with Doctors highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Click on the right person.

<Figure src={img4} alt="Doctors team member list with Mathieu Toussaint highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Click _Add to Discussion_.

<Figure src={img5} alt="Mathieu Toussaint profile with Add to discussion highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### The new participant is now added and is classified under the correct team. If there is an envelope next to their name, it means they have not yet joined the discussion.

<Figure src={img6} alt="Participants panel showing Mathieu Toussaint added with a pending invitation icon" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>

## Step-by-Step Guide to Adding a Participant by Searching Their Name

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

#### Click on the participant circle at the top right of your thread.

<Figure src={img0} alt="Participant circle at the top right of a discussion thread" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### You can use the search bar to look for a specific contact.

<Aside type="note">

Remember to activate the _Contacts_ filter, otherwise only workplaces will be displayed.

</Aside>

<Figure src={img7} alt="Search picker with the Contacts filter highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Select the participant you want to add to the discussion thread.

<Figure src={img8} alt="Contacts search result with Amélie Beauchamp highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Click _Add to Discussion_.
<Figure src={img9} alt="Amélie Beauchamp profile with Add to discussion highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>

## Step-by-Step Guide to Adding a Team

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

#### Click on the participant circle at the top right of your thread.

<Figure src={img0} alt="Participant circle at the top right of a discussion thread" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### To add an entire team so that the message lands in their triage box, click _Add +_ near _Teams_.

</li>

<li>

#### Select the workplace where the team is located.

<Figure src={img10} alt="Workplace picker with Centre de réadaptation Soleil highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### Under the workplace, click _Add to Discussion_.

<Figure src={img11} alt="Workplace profile with Add to discussion highlighted" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

#### At this point, you are offered the teams you can add to the discussion. Select the team of your choice to add it.

<Figure src={img12} alt="Team menu with Orderlies highlighted to add to the discussion" width={650} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>
