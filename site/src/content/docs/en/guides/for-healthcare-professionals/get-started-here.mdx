## Essential Tutorials to Begin

* If you have not yet created your account, do it [here](/en/guides/for-healthcare-professionals/account-creation/independent-account-creation).
* [Add a contact to your directory](/en/guides/for-healthcare-professionals/network/add-contact-to-directory) or [invite one of your colleagues to join the Braver network](/en/guides/for-healthcare-professionals/network/invite-healthcare-professional-to-join-braver-network).
* [Create your first clinical discussion thread](/en/guides/for-healthcare-professionals/discussion-threads/create-clinical-discussion-thread).
* [Close a discussion thread and export it as PDF](/en/guides/for-healthcare-professionals/discussion-threads/close-and-export-discussion-thread-as-pdf) to add to the patient file.
