## Step-by-Step

<Aside type="note">

PILOTS: Did you test the app in the _pre-production_ environment? You'll need to repeat the process when you move to _production_.

</Aside>

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

**In Gustav, click the chat bubble.**

<Figure src={img0} alt="" width={563} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Select&#x20;**_**I don't have a Braver account yet.**_

<Aside type="note">

If you have used Braver on its own or in third-party software, choose the _Use another account_ option to link that existing account with your new team in Gustav.

</Aside>

<Figure src={img1} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**You'll be asked to confirm that you really want to continue without linking an existing Braver account, because this action can't be undone. Click&#x20;**_**OK**_**.**

<Figure src={img2} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**1) Scroll to the bottom, then 2) click&#x20;**_**Accept the terms of use**_ **if you want to continue.**

<Figure src={img3} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**You'll then need to set up a PIN. Click&#x20;**_**Set up**_**.**

<Aside type="note">

This PIN will be useful, among other things, if you want to use Braver on a mobile device.

</Aside>

<Figure src={img4} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**Enter your PIN and repeat it. Both must be identical.**

<Figure src={img5} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

<li>

**You're all set! Your Braver account is now activated!**

<Figure src={img6} alt="" width={375} align="left" />

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>
