# Version 1.11.1

#### 1. New Features

1. You can join a Braver Connect call in your phone's browser without installing the app

#### 2. Improvements

1. In the clinician app, view and edit photos and videos while they upload. File transfers run more smoothly
2. Thread templates can keep their preset participants without requiring a default thread title
3. Forms show accepted formats and size limits before an attachment is added. Returning from a sub-form restores your place
4. On Android, PDF forms are easier to fill out with the keyboard and date picker

#### 3. Fixes

1. Caregivers keep their other patient records when access to one is removed. Their list updates without signing in again
2. Trajectory filters account for your care channel access rights. Management-only roles apply as soon as a channel is created
3. Quebec health insurance numbers and long identifiers display correctly in patient records
4. Admin lists and settings display correctly when you return to a page, including forms and organizational units
5. Braver Connect opens photos and videos for viewing only
6. Form choices show their full text, and fields for attaching multiple files display correctly. Submitted PDF forms include the last entered answer
7. After sharing a form to a new thread, you stay on the page you were on
