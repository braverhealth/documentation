# Version 1.8.3

## New features

* **Message reactions** — You can now add, view, and remove reactions on messages in a discussion. Hover over or long-press a message to open the emoji picker. Reactions are displayed with a count per type, and a tooltip shows the names of participants who reacted along with the date and time of their reaction.

## Bug fixes

* Fixed an infinite loading state in the Braver Connect flow when switching from "Sign In" to "Create Account".
* Fixed an issue where a user's profession was not validated after accepting a network invitation.
